COMPONENT Y / DITHER BLOOM · HERO SPLASH · MOVE THE CURSOR

It follows
the cursor.

A pocket eclipse with a comet tail: a dark disc with a solid rim and a dithered corona that trails the pointer and breathes. Move and the tail streams out behind it, sparks riding it back; stop and it draws in. Left alone it wanders on a slow Lissajous path.

GUIDE / DITHER BLOOM

Overview

A WebGL2 dither field driven by dither.js. The stencil is the crescent at pocket size: a disc whose centre lives in a custom uniform, moved each frame by the stencil's frame hook, which eases toward the pointer while it is over the hero and toward a Lissajous curve when it is not. The same hook keeps a smoothed velocity; its direction and magnitude become a teardrop behind the disc, 80px of tail per pixel-per-frame of speed, filled with dithered odds that thin along its length. Half the sparks fall into the disc, half stream down the tail.

When to use

Heroes that want one interactive moment without a mouse-trail or a cursor replacement. Touch devices never see the pointer, so the idle path is the real design, tune it first.

Spec

Palette
rim #fbcfe8 · mid #ec4899 · far #4a1030 · spark #fff1f7 on #050505
Disc
radius 12% of the short side, breathes ±6%, 4px solid rim, corona at 55% length
Motion
eases 5%/frame toward the target, idle Lissajous 0.0071/0.0093 rad/f
Tail
opposite the velocity (10%/frame smoothed), 80px per px/f, capped at 900px, taper (1−u)^0.6
A11y
aria-hidden canvas, copy ignores the pointer, reduced-motion = one still frame
Perf
WebGL2, 2.2 MP pixel budget, pauses offscreen

Anti-patterns

Do not snap to the pointer; the ease is what makes it feel heavy and gives the tail its length. Do not grow the disc past 15% of the short side, it starts covering the copy. Keep the copy pointer-events: none so the hero gets every move.
