GUIDE / DITHER PORTAL

Overview

A WebGL2 dither field driven by dither.js. The stencil maps each cell into the oval's unit circle. Outside the rim it is the crescent falloff; inside it is a vortex: three logarithmic spiral arms turning about a black core, drawn as mid-tint odds on a floor of far-tint dust, with rim-tint specks where the arms meet the rim. The particles follow the same spiral equation as the arms, so they read as matter riding the current in.

When to use

Heroes with the copy on the left and one object on the right, the crescent's composition with a doorway instead of a horizon. In portrait the portal drops below the copy.

Spec

Palette
rim #c4b5fd · mid #7c3aed · far #2e1065 · spark #f5f3ff on #050505
Oval
centre (72% w, 50% h), tall radius 40% h, aspect 0.68, 4px solid rim
Vortex
3 arms, twist 6, turns 0.025 rad/f, core black under 12% radius
Sparks
born at 1.6 radii, spiral to 0.12 over 250–400 frames, half-bright until they cross the rim
A11y
aria-hidden canvas, pointer-events none, reduced-motion = one still frame
Perf
WebGL2, 2.2 MP pixel budget, pauses offscreen

Anti-patterns

Do not put copy inside the oval; the vortex is busy by design. Do not add arms past five, the spiral turns into noise at 2px cells. Keep the aspect under 0.8 or it stops reading as a standing doorway.
